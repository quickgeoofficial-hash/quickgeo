Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Quickgeo ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Quickgeo website. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our service.

Information We Collect

We collect minimal data. When you visit Quickgeo, standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) may be recorded. We do not require account registration. Preferences such as language and theme settings are stored locally in your browser using localStorage and never transmitted to our servers.

Cookies and Advertising

Quickgeo uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information on how Google uses data, visit How Google uses data.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Fonts for typography and Google Translate API for optional content translation. These services may collect data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. GIF search is powered by Giphy; see Giphy's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

User preferences (theme, language, bookmarks, reactions) are stored in your browser's localStorage and remain entirely on your device. We do not have access to this data.

Children's Privacy

Quickgeo does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users by updating the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Contact page.
