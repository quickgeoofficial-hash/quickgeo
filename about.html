About Quickgeo

Quickgeo is a live news channel dedicated to geopolitics — covering the latest developments in the world.

Our Mission

We believe geospatial knowledge should be accessible to everyone. Quickgeo curates and publishes timely, accurate updates for professionals, researchers, and enthusiasts in the geospatial community.

What We Cover

Our coverage spans breaking geospatial news, satellite imagery releases, GIS software updates, mapping platform changes, open data announcements, and notable industry developments from organisations like ESA, NASA, Google Maps, OpenStreetMap, and more.

How It Works

Posts are published by our editorial team and categorised for easy browsing. Users can react to posts, save them for later, and share updates with their network. The platform is available in 10 languages and supports dark mode for comfortable reading at any time of day.
